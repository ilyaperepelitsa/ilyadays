# Editorial review rubric

Mechanical citation validity and interpretive accuracy are separate. Exact anchors are binary validation results, not fidelity scores. Human reviewers must inspect surrounding context, scope, conditions, modality, negation, counterevidence and the bridge from premises to conclusion.

Use the future ranking dimensions independently. Anchor ordinal assessments as unassessed, limited, substantial or strong; record rationale and assessor/date. Never turn an uncalibrated relevance score into a probability. Personal resonance and clarity are user ratings, independently of fidelity, and begin missing.

Core-principle eligibility requires reviewed evidence and resolved objections before usefulness ranking. Repetition/graph centrality is not truth or moral importance. Two translations of one passage are one underlying occurrence, not independent evidence. Modern applications may belong in a labeled companion section. Historic Stoic conformity remains unassessed without a separate cited reference corpus.

Review each evidence link, explain joint premises, state scope and qualifications, then approve or reject a conclusion. Inspect each expression for added certainty, removed conditions, new advice or moral obligations. A resonant sentence that changes meaning must return to meaning review. A lock protects a deliberate decision until explicitly unlocked.

Synthetic acceptance cases cover different fixture books, negation, conditional advice, same-topic disagreement, a qualifier, recurring phrases, a multi-theme passage, and an unsupported universal promise. They test software behavior only. Actual-corpus evaluation must be human reviewed after import, with held-out examples that are not used for prompt tuning. Track anchor validity, fidelity, retrieval recall, coverage, redundancy and clarity separately.
