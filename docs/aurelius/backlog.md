# Ordered implementation backlog

1. Stage 0 — decisions, edition acquisition ledger, private processing boundary, data schemas/migration, synthetic fixture and implementation log.
2. Stage 1 — usable resizable evidence editor, immutable revisions, exact anchors, manual many-to-many links, joint explanation, expression preview, stale/unsupported states, locks/reviews, local SQLite and synthetic browser persistence. Verify bidirectional click-through and restart.
3. Stage 2 — authorized Hays EPUB/HTML/plain-text ingestion, safe archive/markup processing, exclusion/authorship audit, canonical numbering review, raw/normalized mapping and import approval. Only then tokenize and embed canonical sources/spans with text-embedding-3-large, 3072 dimensions, exact cosine, complete-input/config cache, checkpoints and literal/semantic lookup. PDF later; no OCR.
4. Stage 3 — versioned PCA with explained variance, average-linkage cosine clustering in full dimensions, separate curated multiple-parent concept DAG and overlapping reviewed memberships; preserve manual decisions across reruns.
5. Stage 4 — typed proposition extraction, whole-corpus evidence/qualification/challenge search, coverage matrix, joint inference bundles, Structured Outputs validation and independently recorded critique; human approval gate.
6. Stage 5 — clause mapping and variant comparison, robust provenance marks, focused connectors and separate React Flow exploration, semantic diffs/accessibility/plain mode. Stage 1 provides conservative whole-revision invalidation, not semantic equivalence detection.
7. Stage 6 — anchored ordinal rubrics, separate centrality/foundational/practical/actionability/fidelity/resonance/distinctiveness, configurable weights and missing personal ratings; stable principle IDs, coverage ledger, Markdown/HTML/manifests and rights-aware exports.
8. Stage 7 — bounded jobs, reservation before requests, checkpoints, cancellation, retry/unknown-outcome audit, held-out human evaluation and quality report. Limits cannot be raised by a model. No claim of corpus completion while unresolved items remain.

Real-corpus human-reviewed retrieval/fidelity examples cannot be created before the authorized edition is imported. Synthetic regression cases are scaffolding, not scholarly evaluation. Maintain separate citation validity, fidelity, recall, coverage, redundancy and clarity measures.
