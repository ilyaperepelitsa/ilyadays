Exact words and their functions

1 Qualification attached to an act

“If it be possible” points to “consider.” This is worth preserving. Print them together in every derived instruction, including the single-source fallback.

2 Method or second instruction?

“And reason with thyself” is another imperative. Calling it the method of considering is a plausible reading. Store the coordinated-act reading alongside it until an editor chooses.

3 Question and answer

“The understanding” acquires meaning through the question it answers. Label this link “answers.” The same bare arrow elsewhere means “qualifies” or “is the object of.” An extractor needs those distinctions.

Both branches fail the proposed test

The chosen decision is “Is this verdict sound?” Branch ① tells the reader to inspect the impression. Branch ② identifies who judges. Neither supplies a criterion of soundness. Stopping only branch ② implies that branch ① passed.

The constitution article and its evidence

1 Readable article

First person suits a personal constitution. Keep the title and the article readable as prose. Label the scope of the exercise. “When the insult comes” is firmer than the initial anticipation of a possible encounter.

The article selects an entire sentence. “When,” “ask” and “copy” have different origins inside it. Selecting any of them lights the same bundle. Trace the smallest meaningful claim and show the exact editorial addition.

The inference here names a real addition. Keep it. In other states the generic paraphrase message says only that the editor used first person. It gives the reviewer no account of lost conditions, changed modality or a shift from observation to instruction.
