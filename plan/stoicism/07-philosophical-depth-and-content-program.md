# Philosophical depth and content-development program

## The content problem

The current five-passage, one-conclusion fixture proves a narrow interaction. It does not demonstrate the depth or range expected from an editorial reconstruction of Meditations. Merely adding more equally generic sentences would preserve that problem at a larger scale.

The project should concentrate distinctive ideas, not convert every passage into modern reassurance. The eventual content program must remain grounded in the selected translation and retain uncomfortable implications, qualifications and tensions. Until that corpus is available, examples are original synthetic teaching/interaction fixtures and must be labeled accordingly.

## Demonstration breadth before the real corpus

Build a richer synthetic demonstration around several intellectually distinguishable questions. The following are editorial hypotheses for coverage testing, not a table of contents to impose on the real work:

| Question | Distinction worth demonstrating | Overreach to reject |
|---|---|---|
| Judgment and impressions | An event versus an added evaluative judgment | “All harm is imaginary” |
| Action and social duty | Fair action versus retreat into private calm | “Ignore other people to protect your peace” |
| Mortality and time | Finite attention and conduct under mortality | “Nothing matters because everyone dies” |
| Anger and other people | Understanding error without excusing injustice | “Never object or resist” |
| Desire and outcomes | Directed action with uncertain results | “Never plan or care about consequences” |
| Reputation and recognition | Worth of an action versus external applause | “All criticism is worthless” |
| Nature and wider order | Metaphysical premise versus practical implication | Treating one cosmic claim as settled modern fact |
| Self-discipline and embodied life | Restraint, duty, fatigue and limits | Presenting contempt for the body as health advice |

Each demonstrated teaching needs more than a slogan: situation, precise statement, practical implication, qualification, evidence roles, a nearby rejected inference and a formulation whose resonance can be judged separately from fidelity.

## Synthetic content rules

Write original examples; do not imitate Hays or manufacture quotation-like text under Marcus’s name. Use unmistakable demo references rather than pretending that a synthetic sentence is Book 4.3. Do not silently insert public-domain translations as though they were the selected corpus.

Synthetic examples can illustrate philosophical questions and software behavior, but they do not certify what Marcus teaches. Their sources are fixture records, not scholarly citations. A richer demo should make the eventual workflow meaningful while preserving that boundary prominently.

Include more than one span from a passage where different claims matter. Include a passage with two concept memberships but support for only one particular conclusion. Include conflicting statements on the same subject. Include a genuinely conditional recommendation that becomes false when an editor removes its condition.

## Real-corpus editorial method

After approved import, begin from passages and extracted propositions. Record what is asserted, recommended, questioned or used rhetorically. Avoid treating personal reminders to the author as automatically universal prescriptions for every reader.

Search the whole work for alternative formulations, limits and tensions. Do not let a provisional theme assignment restrict the evidence considered. Corpus-wide proposition review and coverage tracking are required before a core principle claims broad evidential standing.

The final reconstruction is editorial. It is not a newly discovered manuscript or an authoritative definition of all Stoicism. Claims about historical Stoic conformity require a separately cited reference corpus; otherwise they remain tentative or unassessed.

## Distinctiveness and tension review

For each candidate teaching, ask what would be lost if it were removed. If its only content is “be a good person” or “stay calm,” determine whether a more precise claim was lost during paraphrase. If two teachings seem redundant, compare their circumstances, obligations, metaphysical premises and limiting conditions before merging.

Some material may resist a comfortable modern handbook. Do not delete it simply because it is awkward. Preserve disputed, contextualized or intentionally omitted status with a reason. Historical power, social hierarchy, war, death, providence, bodily valuation and obligation may require careful context; context is not permission to invent what the author would have said today.

## Content record for each teaching

A teaching dossier should contain the proposed statement, source-grounded propositions, practical reach, scope, qualifications, counterevidence, bridge assumptions, interpretation type, concise reasoning, reviewer decisions and expression variants.

Mark modern practices, examples and metaphors as such. An exercise that helps a modern reader may be valuable without being a direct ancient instruction. Keep it in the appropriate labeled section rather than retroactively attributing it to the source.

## Content acceptance review

A reviewer should be able to explain how two neighboring teachings differ, which conditions limit each one, and why the final wording has not strengthened the source. A reasonable disagreement can remain open. The application should not reward forced philosophical tidiness.

For the synthetic demo, verify diversity of cases and inspectability, not real-corpus coverage. For the selected corpus, use human-reviewed examples across books, long/short sections, negation, multi-theme material and difficult interpretations. Keep held-out cases outside prompt tuning.

## Immediate delivery target

Replace the one-item showcase with a selectable set of substantial demonstrations, each with several sources, an explicit qualification and at least one failed neighboring inference. Do not reset existing user teachings to install this content. The map and writing desk must use the same versioned records, so selecting an interesting cluster leads to actual inspectable material rather than a decorative label.
