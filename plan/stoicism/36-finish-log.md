# Finishing the project: plan and running log

On 23 November 2026 the owner set the goal **"finish the project fully"**, after being told what was not done: Articles 7 to 38, 353 of 418 passages still only in the 1634 English, Articles 1 to 5 still quoting that English, nothing in Russian, nothing pushed. Earlier the same day they had said "looks good" of the translation pilot and asked "do we also translate to russian right away? or just translate final stuff".

This file is the plan for that run and its log. It continues [35-completion-log.md](35-completion-log.md). The end state is the one in [32-delegation-plan.md](32-delegation-plan.md), section 0, plus the translation.

## Decisions taken for the owner (they said not to stop and ask)

1. **Translate first.** All remaining passages are translated from the Greek before their articles are written, by the three-reader method (article prompt rule 23; briefs in `apps/aurelius/prompts/translation-*.md`).
2. **The 38 sections stay.** The delegation plan puts re-clustering out of scope, and in chapter 1 the grouping held on the new English (56 of 65 passages still nearest their own section). After the whole book is embedded, the same figure is reported per section, and passages that no longer sit nearest their section are listed for the owner. Nothing is moved.
3. **Every article quotes the new English** (`"edition": "project-v1"`), as Article 6 does. Articles 1 to 5 are moved onto it after their sections' articles are otherwise unchanged; the three proposed changes to Marcus's wording in Article 1 stay proposals.
4. **Russian comes last**, from the settled text and from the Greek (rule 24), and its scope is decided when the English is final.
5. **No push without the owner's word.** Pushing publishes the site. Everything is committed locally, one commit per stage.
6. **Paid calls:** only the embedding of the new translation, which the owner asked for, under a stated cap each time.

## How the translation is run

Scratch folder of this session: `scratchpad/wide/` (`crosswalk.py`, `batch.py`, `pairs.py`, `validate.py`, `merge.py`, one folder per section). Per section: a matching of 1634 passages to modern chapters, checked by reading; `greek.json`; `draft.json` (first translator); `check.json` (second reader); `settled.json` (third); then `merge.py`, which appends to `content/aurelius/translation/project-v1.json`. If the scratch folder is lost, the method is in rule 23 and the three briefs, and the store itself records what is done (`sections`).
