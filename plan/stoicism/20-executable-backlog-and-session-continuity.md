## Work package E — concepts and grounded synthesis

### E1. Curate concepts independently

Implement versioned definitions, inclusion/exclusion, multiple parents, explicit overlapping memberships, exclusions, locks and non-hierarchical associations. Keep cluster proposals separate.

### E2. Extract and review propositions

Preserve assertion kind, conditions, modality, negation and exact source anchors. Validate model-proposed references in code and retain failed/ambiguous cases for review.

### E3. Assemble evidence and coverage

Search support, qualifications and challenges across the work. Record inspected material and joint roles. Maintain unresolved coverage instead of declaring top-k results exhaustive.

### E4. Draft, critique and human-review conclusions

Use typed records and a separate rejecting critic. Limit automatic repair. Preserve alternative interpretations and resolved objections. A model cannot approve itself.

Dependencies: D for real-corpus work; fixture interfaces/tests can be developed earlier without pretending that the source has been processed.

## Work package F — expression, ranking and manuscript

### F1. Expression variants and semantic changes

Generate/edit explanatory, concise, reflective and evocative variants from accepted meaning. Track clause mappings and changed scope, certainty, conditions and obligations. Keep clarity/resonance ratings separate from fidelity.

### F2. Transparent ranking

Implement anchored ordinal rubrics, eligibility gates, explicit profile weights, missing personal ratings and explainable component assessments. Manual manuscript order remains an editorial decision.

### F3. Manuscript and coverage

Build stable parts/chapters/principles/blocks, concentration and coverage ledger, compact/handbook/study views, and explicit adoption of expression revisions. Preserve exceptions and distinctive material.

### F4. Export and rights review

Deliver Markdown/standalone HTML and typed JSON exports with provenance appendix/manifests. Separate private-study and shareable policies; do not bundle the full source translation publicly by default.

## Work package G — bounded operation and completion

Implement durable queues, reservations, cancellation, retry/unknown-outcome records, restart, corpus-wide review progress and quality reports. Exercise representative recovery scenarios, including budget exhaustion with unfinished items.

The corpus-completion gate requires either completed bounded work plus review evidence or a precise report of unresolved items. “The worker stopped” is not a completion claim.
