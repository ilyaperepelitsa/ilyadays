# Constitutional structure and comparison corpora

Research checked 2026-11-11. This is a form study for a personal constitution / book scaffold. It does not make Marcus Aurelius a political theorist or imply that a private practice should function like a state law.

## Structural forms worth adapting

| Source | Structural feature | Personal-book adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| [Germany Basic Law, English text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_gg/index.html) | A short preamble, a prominent first-rights section, then separate provisions about how rights apply and how they can be restricted or remedied (Articles 1–19). Later provisions describe amendment; Article 79 is explicitly listed as “Amendment of the Basic Law.” | Open with a brief purpose statement; state core commitments before practice chapters; add an “application and limits” note that distinguishes chosen practice from claims about what Marcus meant; provide a way to correct or retire a practice when evidence or lived experience contradicts it. The translation reflects amendments through 22 March 2025 and may lag the German text; read its [translation notice](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_gg/index.html). |
| [South Africa Constitution, official Department of Justice text](https://www.justice.gov.za/constitution/chp01.html) | A readable chapter/section structure separates founding provisions, the Bill of Rights, government, courts, public administration and amendment. The official site exposes the text by chapter; the Department’s text is copyright-noticed, and the PDF variants include image-only files. | Give principles and habits their own sections, then distinguish daily practices, relationships/duties, and review. Borrow navigation and layered organization only. Do not import the Constitution’s political commitments or copy its prose. |
| [India Constitution, Legislative Department 2025 PDF](https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/359f70a69695affb9d72f8393102bd2e.pdf) | Parts group fundamental rights and fundamental duties separately (Part III and Part IVA, Article 51A); Part XX addresses amendment (Article 368). This makes obligations and revision visible as first-class structures. | A compact “duties to self/others” section can sit beside principles, while an explicit revision process can record what changed and why. Treat these as voluntary commitments, not enforceable rules. The official edition says it is current as of 11 November 2025 and includes amendments through the 106th Amendment; verify current status before future use. |

These are useful as **organizational patterns**: preamble → principles → rights/limits or practices → duties → application/interpretation → revision. The personal version should use first-person, non-coercive language, preserve uncertainty, and keep source evidence beside each interpretive claim. Constitutional articles govern public institutions and citizens; Stoic reflections should remain editorial choices the author can question.

The Swiss Federal Constitution is a potentially useful structural comparator, but the English versions surfaced here are informational translations and Fedlex says English is not an official language. Prefer a current German, French or Italian official text if adding Switzerland to a text-comparison corpus; do not treat the English translation as legally authoritative. See the [Swiss Federal Chancellery language note](https://www.bk.admin.ch/en/english) and [Fedlex language disclaimer](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2009/821/en).

## Existing vector collections: compatibility audit

OpenAI documents `text-embedding-3-large` as supporting up to 3072 dimensions; vectors shortened with the `dimensions` parameter are a different width. See the [official embeddings guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/embeddings). Width alone does not establish comparability: the embedding model, model version/configuration, exact embedded text, prefixes/context, normalization, and chunk boundaries must be known. Vectors produced by different models cannot be compared directly just because they share 3072 dimensions. Re-embedding the desired text with the target model and exact input pipeline is the safe baseline.

| Candidate | What its published metadata establishes | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| [BYU-Idaho Standard Works contextual embeddings](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BYU-Idaho/Contextual-Embeddings) | 41,995 verse rows; `text-embedding-3-large`, 3072 floats; each stored vector encodes generated chapter-context plus the verse, not the verse alone; CC BY 4.0 applies to summaries, embeddings and dataset arrangement; source verse editions are described as public domain. | **Closest verified match in model and width**, but not a like-for-like embedding of a bare canonical passage. Could support a separately labeled scripture comparison experiment if contextual text and attribution are preserved. Do not mix as if equivalent to plain source chunks. |
| [promehedi Quran embeddings](https://huggingface.co/datasets/promehedi/quran_embeddings) | 6,236 ayahs, OpenAI model claimed, embedding sequence shown as 1,536; MIT dataset card metadata conflicts with a non-commercial-use restriction in the README; model ID/version and precise construction are not stated. | **Not compatible as published**: wrong width for the full 3072 configuration and uncertain rights/provenance. Treat model identity, exact vector width, and license as unresolved; do not use pending verification. |
| [dhlak US & Iowa legal embeddings](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dhlak/legal_chroma_embeddings) | Includes 129 U.S. Constitution and 288 Iowa Constitution chunks; `bge-m3` vectors; structure-aware chunks for most collections and LLM segmentation for Iowa law; HF card says license `other`, while README says most source content public domain. | **Not compatible**: different model; cannot compare its vectors to `text-embedding-3-large`. Potential reference for legal-structure chunking only. Dataset and source license details need review before redistribution. |
| [Clarus sacred-text RAG project](https://github.com/aliozdenisik/Clarus) | README claims about 123,000 vectors across Bible/Quran collections, `text-embedding-3-large`, 3072 dimensions, and verse-aware semantic chunking; repo code is MIT but upstream corpus rights are distinct. Its described Qdrant index is project/runtime data; a downloadable, reusable vector artifact and a complete per-vector input/config manifest were not verified. | **Promising lead, not verified importable data.** Same stated model/dimensions is not enough to reuse without artifact access and exact provenance/chunk text. |

One related public project, [bible-llm-reference](https://github.com/prive8/bible-llm-reference), reports a built 66,689-passage `text-embedding-3-large` index at 3072 dimensions, but explicitly says built indexes are gitignored. It is useful evidence that same-model religious indexes exist, not a vector dataset currently downloadable from that repository. Its licensing notes also flag some source translations as rights-uncertain.

### Gaps and next steps

- No constitution-specific vector corpus using `text-embedding-3-large` at full 3072 dimensions was verified in this bounded search.
- The BYU contextual scripture dataset is the only reviewed candidate with strong published evidence for same model, full dimensions, chunk granularity, and a dataset license. Context construction differs from plain passages, so preserve that distinction and rerun a retrieval check before any integration.
- Existing same-model vectors still need provenance beyond model name and width: exact model identifier/version, request dimensions, input text/hash, context/prefix recipe, chunk IDs and boundaries, normalization, date, and source-text licensing.
- No paid embeddings/API calls or third-party full corpora were added. Two small official text-readable samples are stored locally under the ignored `.aurelius/comparisons/` directory; their URLs/access and rights notes are recorded in `content/aurelius/comparisons/registry.json`. They are research inspection copies, not approved redistribution artifacts.
