# MooKEN-inspired caramel mini cream puffs

An original home adaptation of the small caramel-crusted custard puffs in the user's Osaka Reddit reference. The reference photo and an independent cross-section were inspected; the shop's formula and manufacturing method remain unverified. This recipe has not been kitchen tested. It is a vanilla choux recipe with a thin caramel-craquelin topping, not a claim to reproduce proprietary quantities exactly.

The recipe yields about 24 small puffs for six people. It includes three component ingredient lists, three illustrated ingredient previews and 12 separately illustrated stages: cook custard, chill custard, caramel, cookie discs, panade, egg consistency, piping, frozen topping, staged baking, venting/drying, underside filling and serving. Explicit texture checks accompany the temperatures and times. Conventional and fan settings are distinguished; serving scaling leaves the individual puff size unchanged. Custard cooling overlaps the shell work.

Canonical authoring lives in `../recipes/src/mooken_cream_puffs.{py,json}` and `src/i18n/ru/mooken-mini-cream-puffs.json`, with images specified in `../recipes/imagegen/recipes/mooken_mini_cream_puffs.py`. All were made with the built-in image tool and individually reviewed at full size. The hero reuses the final plated scene. `requirements.json` snapshots the unchanged shared image policy and the approved nigiri style reference.
