Oyakodon

“Parent-and-child” rice bowl. The chicken sits in the sauce — kombu, dashi, soy, mirin, sake and sugar — while finely chopped ginger and onion soften in the pan with a pinch of salt. Then the chicken goes in with all of its marinade, gets bound with barely set egg poured in two stages, and slides over hot rice.

1 bowl~35 min (+ rice)

If your rice is already cooked, skip the first bar — about 35 minutes, most of it the chicken marinating.

Step by step

  1. 1Rice first

    ~45 min ahead

    For this step

    • Cooked short-grain rice1 bowl
    • Have a bowl of hot rice ready before you start the pan — the egg won’t wait.
    • Start the rice cooker about 45 minutes ahead, or reheat leftover rice until steaming.

  2. 2Marinate the chicken

    ~5 min + 15–30 min

    For this step

    • Chicken thigh fillets150 g
    • Kombua 5 cm square
    • Trim the thigh fillets of any excess fat and cut them into 2–3 cm pieces.
    • In a bowl, stir until the sugar dissolves:
      • dashi 80 ml
      • soy sauce 4 tbsp
      • mirin 1 tbsp
      • sake 1 tbsp
      • sugar 1 tsp
    • Drop in the kombu, then the chicken.
    • Leave it 15–30 minutes (in the fridge if longer).

  3. 3Onion and ginger

    ~5 min

    For this step

    • Onion½ small (~60 g)
    • Gingera 2 cm piece (~8 g)
    • Onion: cut off both ends (root and tip), halve it through the cut ends and peel.
    • Lay each half flat and slice it lengthwise — perpendicular to the cut ends, along the lines — about 5 mm thick. Slicing across the lines gives rings that fall apart; lengthwise slices hold their shape in the pan.
    • Ginger: peel, cut into thin matchsticks, then across into tiny cubes.

  4. 4Beat the eggs

    ~1 min

    For this step

    • Eggs2
    • Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat loosely — 4–5 strokes with chopsticks, cutting through the whites.
    • You want streaks of white and yolk, not a uniform yellow.

  5. 5Ginger, then onion

    medium-low~3 min

    For this step

    • Neutral oil1 tsp
    • Gingera 2 cm piece (~8 g)
    • Onion½ small (~60 g)
    • Salta pinch
    • Small pan, the oil, medium-low heat.
    • Drop in the ginger for ~30 seconds until fragrant, then add the onion and a pinch of salt.
    • Stir until the onion is a bit soft — 2–3 minutes, no colour.

  6. 6Let it cool down

    heat off~5 min

    Take the pan off the heat and let it cool for a few minutes while the chicken finishes marinating.

  7. 7Chicken in, with all the marinade

    medium5–6 min
    • Back on medium.
    • Tip in the chicken with all of its marinade (kombu too), spread it in a single layer.
    • Bring to a simmer, cover, and simmer, turning once, until cooked through with no pink inside.

  8. 8Pour ⅔ of the egg

    medium30–60 s
    • Fish out the kombu.
    • Drizzle about two-thirds of the egg in a circle over the chicken and onion.
    • Cover and cook until the egg at the edges is set but the top is still runny.

  9. 9Pour the rest, heat off

    heat off~45 s
    • Pour the remaining egg, cook 10–15 seconds, then turn off the heat and cover for 30 seconds.
    • The egg should be glossy and just set.
    • Cook a little longer covered if you prefer it firm.

  10. 10Slide onto the rice

    For this step

    • Spring onion greens or mitsubaa few
    • Shichimi togarashioptional
    • Tilt the pan and slide everything, broth included, over the rice.
    • Top with spring onion greens or mitsuba and a shake of shichimi.

Sous vide chicken shortcut

  • Cook the breast in the bath first (63 °C, 60–75 min for ~2 cm), then slice it.
  • Skip marinating: after the ginger and onion (step 5), pour in the sauce, bring it to a simmer and add the cooked chicken for just 1 minute — no longer, or it tightens — then go straight to the egg.

Dashi

  • Homemade is best — see niboshi–bonito dashi.
  • Water plus ¼ tsp dashi powder is the quick version; the kombu in the marinade adds depth either way.

Soft eggs

  • The eggs end up only just set.
  • If anyone eating should avoid undercooked egg, keep the lid on with the heat low for another minute or two until fully set.
