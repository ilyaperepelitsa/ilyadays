Noodles first (all three)

  • Follow the packet’s stir-fry preparation instructions, stopping while they still have a little firmness.
  • Drain well and use promptly; they’ll finish cooking in the sauce.
  • Avoid fully softening them beforehand or aggressively stirring them in the pan.

Extra shopping for all three

  • Beyond the basics:
    • oyster sauce
    • dark soy sauce
    • fish sauce
    • tamarind purée
    • mushrooms
    • bok choy
    • spring onions
    • eggs
    • bean sprouts
    • roasted peanuts
    • lime
  • Bok choy can cover both of the first two dishes, so there’s no need to buy separate greens.
