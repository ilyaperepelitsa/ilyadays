Tamagoyaki

Japanese rolled omelet, built from thin layers that are rolled up one after another. Yours is seasoned with sake, mirin and brown sugar, with nori flakes sprinkled on a middle layer so every slice shows a green swirl. Keep the heat moderate: the sugar browns quickly.

1 roll · 2 sides~15 min

Ingredients

Egg mixture

  • Eggs3
  • Mirin1 tbsp
  • Sake1 tsp
  • Brown sugar2 tsp
  • Salta pinch
  • Dashi1–2 tbsp, optional

For the pan

  • Green nori flakes1–2 tsp, for the middle layer
  • Neutral oila little, on a paper towel

Equipment

  • Tamagoyaki pan (rectangular), or a small non-stick frying pan
  • Chopsticks or a thin spatula
  • Folded paper towel for oiling
  • Bamboo mat, optional (for shaping)

Step by step

  1. 1Mix

    3 min

    For this step

    • Eggs3
    • Beat the eggs with the seasonings until the sugar dissolves:
      • mirin 1 tbsp
      • sake 1 tsp
      • brown sugar 2 tsp
      • salt a pinch
      • dashi 1–2 tbsp
    • Cut through the whites with chopsticks rather than whipping — you don’t want foam.

  2. 2Heat and oil the pan

    medium-low2 min

    For this step

    • Neutral oila little
    • Heat the pan on medium-low and wipe a thin film of oil over it with a folded paper towel.
    • A drop of egg should sizzle gently, not spit.

  3. 3First layer

    medium-low~1 min
    • Pour in about a quarter of the egg and tilt to cover the pan.
    • Pop any big bubbles.
    • When the bottom is set but the top is still a little wet, roll it from the far end towards you.

  4. 4Push back and re-oil

    medium-low

    Push the roll back to the far end and wipe oil over the empty part of the pan.

  5. 5Second layer, with nori

    medium-low~1 min

    For this step

    • Green nori flakes1–2 tsp
    • Pour in the next quarter and lift the roll so egg runs underneath it.
    • Sprinkle the nori flakes over this layer, then roll towards you again.

  6. 6Repeat

    medium-low2–3 min
    • Push, oil and pour with the rest of the egg — usually one or two more layers.
    • Each time, lift the roll so the new egg runs underneath it before rolling — that’s what glues the layers together.

  7. 7Shape and rest

    5 min
    • Tip the roll onto a bamboo mat (or a paper towel), wrap it and press gently into a neat block.
    • Leave it for 5 minutes to set.

  8. 8Slice

    Cut into 2 cm slices — the nori shows as a green swirl.

Amounts

  • The seasoning amounts are a starting point — your notes didn’t include quantities.
  • More sugar browns faster, so lower the heat if you sweeten it more.

No tamagoyaki pan?

A small round non-stick pan works; trim the rounded ends after rolling.

Dashi

A spoon or two of dashi makes it softer and juicier (dashimaki style); leave it out for a firmer, sweeter omelet.
