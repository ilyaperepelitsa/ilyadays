Rice noodles

Chicken Pad See Ew

Soy-rich, lightly sweet noodles with egg and greens. Browning the noodles separately from the other ingredients is the key technique here.

1 generous serving~20 min
IngredientsMise en placePrep orderSteps

Ingredients

Main

  • Dried flat rice noodles100 g
  • Chicken150 g, thinly sliced
  • Chinese broccoli or bok choy125 g, chopped; stems and leaves separated
  • Egg1
  • Garlic2 cloves, finely chopped
  • Neutral cooking oil1½ tbsp (½ + 1)

Sauce — mix together

  • Oyster sauce1 tbsp
  • Regular soy sauce1½ tsp
  • Dark soy sauce1 tsp
  • Rice vinegar1 tsp
  • Sugar1 tsp

Equipment

  • Large frying pan or wok (non-stick helps the noodles brown without sticking)
  • Plate

Shift the noodle bar to match your packet: they should be drained just before the browning step.

Step by step

  1. 1Noodles first

    per packet

    For this step

    • Dried flat rice noodles100 g
    • Follow the packet’s stir-fry preparation instructions, stopping while they still have a little firmness.
    • Drain well and use promptly; they’ll finish cooking in the sauce.
    • Avoid fully softening them beforehand or aggressively stirring them in the pan.

  2. 2Prep everything

    ~8 min

    For this step

    • Chicken150 g
    • Chinese broccoli or bok choy125 g
    • Garlic2 cloves
    • Egg1
    • Slice the chicken thinly.
    • Slice the greens’ stems thinly and keep the leaves separate.
    • Chop the garlic and crack the egg into a cup.
    • Mix the sauce:
      • oyster sauce 1 tbsp
      • regular soy sauce 1½ tsp
      • dark soy sauce 1 tsp
      • rice vinegar 1 tsp
      • sugar 1 tsp

  3. 3Chicken and greens

    medium-high~4 min
    • Heat ½ tablespoon oil over medium-high heat.
    • Cook the chicken until nearly done.
    • Add the thinly sliced stems, then garlic and leaves; cook until the chicken is cooked through and greens are tender.

  4. 4Egg

    medium-high~1 min

    Push everything aside, scramble the egg in the cleared space, then transfer everything to a plate.

  5. 5Brown the noodles

    medium-high1–2 min
    • Add the remaining 1 tablespoon oil, the noodles and the sauce.
    • Spread out, leave briefly to brown, then gently turn.
    • Cook for roughly 1–2 minutes.

  6. 6Toss and serve

    medium-high~30 s

    Return the chicken mixture, toss briefly and serve.

No dark soy?

Substitute the same amount of regular soy; the noodles will be paler.
