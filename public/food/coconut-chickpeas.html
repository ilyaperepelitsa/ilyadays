Shivam’s coconut chickpeas

What makes this work is the order: each layer gets its own moment in the pot. The tomato paste cooks until its oil splits out, and the spinach waits until the very end. Spices are measured by feel — go light at first and add more later.

~4 servings~45 min
IngredientsMise en placePrep orderSteps

Ingredients

Main components

  • Chickpeas2 cans (2 × ~400 g), drained — or the same from dried beans
  • Onion1 medium, diced
  • Tomatoes2, chopped
  • Tomato paste2–3 tbsp
  • Coconut milk1 can (~400 ml), full fat recommended
  • Spinacha big handful, fresh or frozen
  • Sweet potato1, cubed — optional but recommended
  • Saltto taste

Aromatics & spices

  • Neutral oilfor the pot (vegetable, canola or coconut)
  • Cumin seeds1–2 tsp
  • Fresh gingera thumb-size piece, minced
  • Garlic4–6 cloves, minced (or make ginger-garlic paste)
  • Turmericvibes — maybe ½–1 tsp
  • Chili powderto taste — ½–2 tsp depending on heat

For serving

  • Ricebasmati, jasmine or brown
  • Naan or roti
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Lime wedges

Equipment

  • Large pot or deep skillet with a lid
  • Wooden spoon
  • Knife and cutting board

Step by step

  1. 1Bloom the aromatics

    medium~7 min

    For this step

    • Neutral oilfor the pot
    • Cumin seeds1–2 tsp
    • Warm the oil in a large pot or deep skillet on medium.
    • Drop in the cumin seeds and give them about 30 seconds to sizzle and open up.
    • Add:
      • onion 1 medium
      • fresh ginger a thumb-size piece
      • garlic 4–6 cloves
    • Let the onion go soft and see-through, 5–7 minutes — give it the full time.

  2. 2Build the spice base

    medium~1 min

    For this step

    • Turmericvibes — maybe ½–1 tsp
    • Chili powderto taste — ½–2 tsp depending on heat
    • Sprinkle in the turmeric and chili powder.
    • Keep stirring for 30–60 seconds while they toast; stop before they smell scorched.
    • Splash in a little water if they start to stick.

  3. 3Cook down the tomato paste

    medium3–5 min

    For this step

    • Tomato paste2–3 tbsp
    • Stir in the tomato paste and keep it moving on medium.
    • In 3–5 minutes the oil splits out and gathers in a shiny ring around the paste.
    • By then the paste is a shade darker and smells deep and savoury.

  4. 4Deglaze with fresh tomatoes

    medium3–4 min

    For this step

    • Tomatoes2
    • Tip in the chopped tomatoes and scrape the stuck bits off the bottom with them.
    • Stir for 3–4 minutes as they collapse into a sauce.

  5. 5Add the coconut milk

    high~3 min

    For this step

    • Coconut milk1 can (~400 ml)
    • Saltto taste
    • Turn the heat up high and empty in the can of coconut milk.
    • Stir it through and let it come to a full boil.
    • Salt it at this point.

  6. 6Add the starches

    medium-low15–20 min
    • When it boils, drop the heat to medium-low.
    • Add:
      • chickpeas 2 cans (2 × ~400 g)
      • sweet potato 1
    • Stir so everything is coated, then simmer 15–20 minutes, until a fork slides into the sweet potato.
    • Loosen with water if it thickens too much. It’s ready when the sweet potato is soft, the chickpeas are hot and the sauce has body.

  7. 7Finish with spinach

    medium-low1–4 min

    For this step

    • Spinacha big handful
    • Stir in the spinach just until it collapses — 1–2 minutes if fresh, 3–4 if frozen.
    • Taste, then fix the salt and chili.
    • Heat off.

  8. 8Serve

    For this step

    • Ricebasmati
    • Naan or roti
    • Spoon it over rice and top with:
      • fresh cilantro
      • lime wedges
    • Naan on the side is good for mopping up.

Spicing by feel

  • Use less than you think at first; more can always go in.
  • Taste once the coconut milk is in and correct the spices before adding the chickpeas.
  • If you want numbers to start from:
    • 1 tsp turmeric
    • 1 tsp chili powder
    • 1–2 tsp cumin seeds
    • 1 inch (~2.5 cm) fresh ginger
    • 4–5 cloves garlic
  • No two pots come out quite the same, and that’s fine.

Swaps and extras

  • More protein:
    • paneer cubes (vegetarian)
    • tofu cubes (vegan)
    • hard-boiled eggs, added at the end
  • In place of sweet potato:
    • potato
    • cauliflower
    • butternut squash
  • In place of spinach:
    • kale
    • chard
    • mustard greens
  • Richer:
    • a spoonful of cashew butter
    • a knob of butter to finish (if not vegan)
    • half coconut cream, half coconut milk
  • As a dal: swap in red lentils (20–25 min); it comes out looser, almost a soup — finish with lemon juice.
  • Other spices:
    • 1 tsp garam masala at the end
    • curry leaves in with the cumin seeds
    • kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)

Storage

  • Keeps 4–5 days in a sealed box in the fridge, or 3 months frozen.
  • Warm it on the stove with a little water.
  • It’s often better on day two.

Good with

Basmati rice, naan or roti, papadum, cucumber raita, mango chutney, lime pickle.
