Shiitake–kombu dashi

The vegan dashi: kombu gives a clean, savoury base and dried shiitake add a deep, earthy one. Cold brewing overnight is the easiest and gentlest way — nothing to watch. Don’t throw out the shiitake afterwards: slice them for the napa cabbage & shiitake egg bowl.

1 litre5 min + overnight (or ~45 min hot)

Overnight is ~8 h; anywhere from 8 to 24 h works. Quick version instead: 30 min soak + ~15 min gentle heat (steps 5–6).

Step by step

  1. Cold brew (overnight)

  2. 1Wipe the kombu

    1 min

    For this step

    • Kombu10 g
    • Wipe the kombu lightly with a damp cloth to remove grit.
    • Don’t wash it — the white powder on the surface is flavour, not mould.

  3. 2Soak overnight

    8–24 h
    • Into a jar, everything cold:
      • kombu 10 g
      • dried shiitake 4–5 (about 15 g)
      • water 1 l
    • Lid on, into the fridge for 8–24 hours.

  4. 3Strain

    5 min
    • Lift out the kombu and shiitake.
    • Squeeze the shiitake gently over the jar to keep their liquid, then pour the dashi through a fine sieve.

  5. 4Keep the shiitake

    • Trim off the tough stems and slice the caps — they’re the main ingredient in the napa cabbage & shiitake egg bowl.
    • The kombu can be brewed once more for a lighter dashi.

  6. Quick version (same day)

  7. 5Quick version: soak 30 minutes

    30 min
    • Into a saucepan, everything cold:
      • kombu 10 g
      • dried shiitake 4–5 (about 15 g)
      • water 1 l
    • Soak for at least 30 minutes (longer is better).

  8. 6Heat gently, kombu out before it boils

    low10–15 min
    • Heat on low. When tiny bubbles appear at the edge (around 60–70 °C, ~10–15 minutes), take the kombu out — boiled kombu turns slimy and bitter.
    • Simmer the shiitake a few more minutes, then strain as in step 3.

Storage

  • Fridge in a closed jar for 3–4 days.
  • Freezes well in ice-cube trays — a cube or two is perfect for a single bowl.

Amounts

  • 10 g kombu + 4–5 shiitake per litre is a standard starting point.
  • For a stronger dashi, add another shiitake or two.
