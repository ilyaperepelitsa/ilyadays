Vegan crunch wrap supreme

Have everything made and laid out before the first wrap — assembly goes quickly, and speed is what gets you a tight, crisp seal. The cheese sauce is potato and carrot blended smooth.

4 large wraps~1 h
IngredientsMise en placePrep orderSteps

Ingredients

Vegan nacho cheese

  • Russet potato1½ cups (~360 ml) peeled and chopped (1 medium)
  • Carrots1 cup (~240 ml) peeled and chopped (2–3 carrots)
  • Unsweetened non-dairy milk2 cups (~480 ml)
  • Nutritional yeast½ cup (~120 ml)
  • Olive oil3 tbsp
  • Lemon juice1 tbsp
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Onion powder½ tsp
  • Garlic powder½ tsp
  • Smoked paprika½ tsp
  • Cornstarch1 tbsp
  • Diced green chiles4 oz can (~115 g), drained — optional, for the nacho version

Taco “meat” (choose one)

  • Black beans1 can, drained and mashed with taco seasoning
  • Vegan crumbles1 package (Beyond, Impossible, etc.)
  • Walnuts1 cup (~240 ml), pulsed with taco seasoning and sautéed

Taco seasoning (if making from scratch)

  • Chili powder1 tbsp
  • Cumin1 tsp
  • Paprika1 tsp
  • Garlic powder, for the seasoning½ tsp
  • Onion powder, for the seasoning½ tsp
  • Oregano¼ tsp
  • Cayennea pinch, optional
  • Salt & pepperto taste

Wraps

  • Large flour tortillas4 (burrito size)
  • Small flour tortillas or tostadas4 (taco size)

Fresh components

  • Vegan sour cream½ cup (~120 ml)
  • Shredded lettuce1 cup (~240 ml)
  • Tomato1 large, diced
  • Salsa½ cup (~120 ml)
  • Guacamole1 cup (~240 ml), or 2 avocados, mashed
  • Fresh cilantro¼ cup (~60 ml)

Equipment

  • Medium pot
  • Blender
  • Large skillet or griddle
  • Spatula
  • Cutting board and knife
  • Food processor (only for the walnut “meat”)

Step by step

  1. 1Make the nacho cheese

    ~20 min

    For this step

    • Russet potato1½ cups (~360 ml) peeled and chopped (1 medium)
    • Carrots1 cup (~240 ml) peeled and chopped (2–3 carrots)
    • Unsweetened non-dairy milk2 cups (~480 ml)
    • Nutritional yeast½ cup (~120 ml)
    • Olive oil3 tbsp
    • Lemon juice1 tbsp
    • Salt1 tsp
    • Onion powder½ tsp
    • Garlic powder½ tsp
    • Smoked paprika½ tsp
    • Cornstarch1 tbsp
    • Diced green chiles4 oz can (~115 g)
    • Cover the potato and carrot with water in a medium pot and bring it to a boil.
    • Give them about 10 minutes, until a fork goes in easily.
    • Meanwhile, load the blender with the rest of the cheese ingredients (plus the chiles, if using).
    • Drain the vegetables, add them to the blender and run it until there are no lumps at all.
    • Thin with a splash more non-dairy milk if it’s too thick. Keep it warm.

  2. 2Make the taco filling

    medium~10 min

    For this step

    • Black beans1 can
    • Vegan crumbles1 package
    • Walnuts1 cup (~240 ml)
    • For homemade seasoning, stir together:
      • chili powder 1 tbsp
      • cumin 1 tsp
      • paprika 1 tsp
      • garlic powder, for the seasoning ½ tsp
      • onion powder, for the seasoning ½ tsp
      • oregano ¼ tsp
      • cayenne a pinch
      • salt & pepper to taste
    • Beans: mash them, season, and heat through.
    • Crumbles: follow the packet.
    • Walnuts: pulse in a food processor, then fry with the seasoning.

  3. 3Prep the fresh stuff

    ~10 min

    For this step

    • Tomato1 large
    • Shredded lettuce1 cup (~240 ml)
    • Guacamole1 cup (~240 ml)
    • Fresh cilantro¼ cup (~60 ml)
    • Dice the tomato.
    • Shred the lettuce.
    • Mash or make the guacamole.
    • Chop the cilantro.

  4. 4Assemble

    ~2 min each

    For this step

    • Large flour tortillas4 (burrito size)
    • Small flour tortillas or tostadas4 (taco size)
    • Vegan sour cream½ cup (~120 ml)
    • Salsa½ cup (~120 ml)
    • Build each wrap from the bottom up:
      • a large tortilla laid flat
      • 2–3 tbsp nacho cheese in the middle
      • ⅓–½ cup (~80–120 ml) taco filling over the cheese
      • a tostada or small tortilla for crunch
      • a layer of sour cream on that
      • tomatoes, lettuce, salsa
      • guacamole and cilantro
    • Go easy on the filling — an overstuffed wrap won’t close.

  5. 5Fold the wrap

    ~1 min each
    • Bring one section of the edge up over the middle.
    • Work your way round, laying each pleat over the one before.
    • It closes into a six- or eight-sided parcel with nothing showing.
    • If the tortilla cracks or won’t close, 10 seconds in the microwave softens it.

  6. 6Toast seam-side down

    medium4–6 min each
    • Heat a dry skillet or griddle on medium.
    • Lay the wrap in seam-side down and press lightly with a spatula.
    • After 2–3 minutes, when it’s golden and crisp, turn it over gently and give it 2–3 more.
    • A light brush of oil on the outside makes it crisper, if you like.

  7. 7Serve

    2 min
    • Rest it 1–2 minutes before cutting.
    • Cut corner to corner so the layers show.
    • Put out salsa, hot sauce or more cheese sauce for dipping.

Working tips

  • Set every component out within reach before you start, and build several in a row.
  • Cracking tortillas need a little warmth; the pleat gets easier after the first one.
  • A tostada is the crunchiest middle but the hardest to bite; a small tortilla crisped in a pan is gentler. Crushed tortilla chips work too.

Ahead of time

  • Wraps can be built in the morning, wrapped in foil and chilled, then toasted to order.
  • The cheese sauce lasts 4–5 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen (whisk it smooth after thawing).
  • Cooked wraps freeze well and reheat in the oven.

Cheese sauce

  • Less milk for a thicker sauce, more for a pourable one.
  • A pinch of turmeric deepens the colour; jalapeños, chipotle powder or hot sauce bring heat.

Other fillings

  • Breakfast: tofu scramble, vegan sausage, hash browns as the crunch.
  • Buffalo cauliflower with ranch.
  • BBQ jackfruit with coleslaw and crispy onions.
