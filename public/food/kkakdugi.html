Kkakdugi (radish cube kimchi)

Radish cubes take a long soak in sweetened salt water, then get tossed in a chili-red seasoning paste. Made your way: coarse salt, garlic, ginger and pear grated on a ceramic grater, and the pear and ginger juice squeezed out through cheesecloth. See also the cabbage kimchi.

4 servings~30 min work + 5 h soaking + 5–7 days in the fridge
IngredientsMise en placePrep orderSteps

Ingredients

Radish

  • Korean radish (mu) or daikon1 kg
  • Green onions (or chives)100 g

Brine

  • Water600 ml
  • Coarse (kosher) salt50 ml
  • Sugar3 tbsp

Filling

  • Korean chili powder (gochugaru)5 tbsp
  • Salted anchovy sauce (or fish sauce)4 tbsp
  • Kelp (kombu) stock4 tbsp
  • Pear juice4 tbsp (grated pear, squeezed through cheesecloth)
  • Crushed garlic2 tbsp
  • Salted shrimp sauce, chopped (or fish sauce)1 tbsp
  • Ginger juice2 tsp (grated ginger, squeezed through cheesecloth)

  • Colander
  • Chef's knife + cutting board
  • Ceramic grater (preferably) or a fine metal one
  • Cheesecloth
  • Mixing bowl
  • Disposable gloves for mixing
  • Airtight container

The soak bar stands for about 5 hours; the fridge bar keeps going for 5–7 days.

Step by step

  1. 1Cube and soak the radish

    ~15 min + 5 h

    For this step

    • Korean radish (mu) or daikon1 kg
    • Stir until the salt and sugar dissolve:
      • water 600 ml
      • coarse (kosher) salt 50 ml
      • sugar 3 tbsp
    • Peel the radish and cut it into 3 cm cubes.
    • Leave the cubes in the brine for around 5 hours, then tip them into a colander.

  2. 2Cut the green onions

    ~3 min

    For this step

    • Green onions (or chives)100 g

    Chop the green onions into short 2 cm pieces.

  3. 3Make the filling

    ~12 min
    • Grate the garlic, ginger and pear on the ceramic grater (or a fine metal one).
    • Squeeze the grated pear and the grated ginger through cheesecloth — the juice is what you want.
    • Mix into a thick red paste:
      • crushed garlic 2 tbsp
      • ginger juice 2 tsp
      • salted anchovy sauce (or fish sauce) 4 tbsp
      • Korean chili powder (gochugaru) 5 tbsp
      • pear juice 4 tbsp
      • salted shrimp sauce, chopped (or fish sauce) 1 tbsp
      • kelp (kombu) stock 4 tbsp

  4. 4Mix

    ~5 min
    • Tip the drained cubes into the paste with the green onions and toss until each cube is red all over.
    • Gloves help — the chili stains.

  5. 5Into the fridge

    5–7 days
    • Pack it into a container with a tight lid and let it ferment in the fridge for 5–7 days before you eat it.
    • Around 5 °C is the ideal fridge temperature for kimchi.

Source

Based on the recipe in Easy Korean Cooking, made with your methods (coarse salt, ceramic grater, cheesecloth for the juices).

Which radish

  • Autumn and winter radish is sweet and mild.
  • Radish from other seasons can taste bitter — add a little extra sugar to balance it.

Salted anchovy and shrimp sauce

Fish sauce can stand in for either, though the Korean sauces taste better and are worth seeking out.

Kelp stock

Plain kombu stock — see the kombu dashi page, or soak a piece of kombu in water.

Storage

Kimchi keeps best in a cold fridge, about 5 °C.
