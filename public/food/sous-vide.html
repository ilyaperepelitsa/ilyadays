Vegetables

FoodStateTempTimeNotes
Asparagus (~1 cm spears)Frozen85°C12–18 min15 min default. Use 18–20 min if clumped/tightly packed.
Asparagus (~1 cm spears)Thawed85°C8–12 minMostly heat-through + slight tenderizing. Single layer cooks best.
Cauliflower (florets, ~5 cm)Thawed85°C30–45 minBag with butter + curry powder. Single layer; weigh down if needed.
Cauliflower (florets, ~5 cm)Frozen85°C45–60 minAdd ~15 min for frozen. Same bag additions.

Asparagus finish (optional): dry well, then blister 30–60 sec in a very hot pan.

Cauliflower finish (optional): drain liquid, then roast or broil 4–6 min for color. Pairs well with a knob of butter and ½–1 tsp curry powder per ~300 g in the bag.

Beets: 85 °C, 1½–4 h by size — see Sous vide beets.
