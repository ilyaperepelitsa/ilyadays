Karaage

Japanese fried chicken. Poking the thighs lets the soy–sake–mirin marinade in; tapioca starch (not flour) with little fork-made crumbs gives the craggy crunch; and frying twice — pale first, rest, then dark — makes it crisp without drying it out.

2 servings (500 g thighs)~1 h incl. 30 min marinating

One batch shown. Fry in batches that don’t crowd the pot — while one batch rests, the next does its first fry. Let the oil come back to 180 °C between batches.

Step by step

  1. 1Cut and poke

    ~8 min

    For this step

    • Boneless chicken thighs, skin on500 g
    • Cut the deboned thighs into 4–5 cm pieces.
    • Poke them all over with a sharp fork or skewer — both sides — so the marinade gets in.

  2. 2Marinate

    30 min – overnight
    • Mix the marinade:
      • ginger, grated 10 g (2 tsp)
      • garlic, grated 2 cloves
      • mirin 1 tbsp
      • soy sauce 2 tbsp
      • sake 1 tbsp
      • ground pepper ¼ tsp
      • oyster sauce 1 tsp
      • brown sugar 1 tsp
    • Toss the chicken in it and marinate in the fridge.

  3. 3Pour off, then flour + starch

    ~3 min

    For this step

    • Flour (into the chicken)1 tbsp
    • Tapioca starch (into the chicken)1 tbsp
    • Pour off and discard the excess marinade.
    • Mix 1 tbsp flour and 1 tbsp tapioca starch into the chicken until every piece has a tacky coat.

  4. 4Starch board and crumbs

    ~5 min

    For this step

    • Tapioca starch (on the board)~80 g
    • Water or leftover marinade (for crumbs)1–2 tsp
    • Spread the tapioca starch over a cutting board.
    • Make the little round bits: drizzle a teaspoon or two of water (or leftover marinade) over part of it and work it with a fork into small crumbs and pebbles.

  5. 5Heat the oil

    medium-high~10 min

    For this step

    • Neutral oilenough for 3–4 cm depth

    Heat 3–4 cm of oil in a heavy pot to 180 °C.

  6. 6Roll in starch

    ~4 min

    While the oil heats, roll each piece in the starch and crumbs on the board, pressing so the bits stick.

  7. 7First fry — pale

    180 °C oil~2–3 min
    • Lower in a batch without crowding.
    • Fry a couple of minutes until light yellow / light orange.

  8. 8Rest

    3–4 min
    • Lift onto a rack for a few minutes.
    • The heat inside keeps cooking the chicken while the oil comes back up to temperature.

  9. 9Second fry — dark and crisp

    180 °C oil~1 min
    • Back in the oil until deeper golden and crisp.
    • Drain on the rack.

  10. 10Serve

    For this step

    • Lemonwedges
    • Japanese mayoto dip

    Serve hot with lemon wedges and Japanese mayo.

The “round bits”

  • Your notes mention pre-making round things with a fork.
  • Read here as: wetting a bit of the starch and forking it into small crumbs, which stick to the chicken and fry into extra-crunchy nubs.
  • Adjust if you did it differently.

Amounts

  • You gave the ingredients, not quantities — these are starting points.
  • Taste the marinade: it should be salty-sweet and strongly gingery.

Is it cooked?

  • Chicken is safe at 74 °C in the centre.
  • The rest between fries finishes the inside; if unsure, cut the biggest piece open after the second fry.

Oil safety

  • Never fill the pot more than half full, keep a lid nearby, dry the pieces of excess marinade, and never put water on burning oil.
  • Let the oil cool completely before straining or discarding it.
