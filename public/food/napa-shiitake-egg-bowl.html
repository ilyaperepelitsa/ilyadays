Napa cabbage & shiitake egg bowl

Built exactly like oyakodon, with the chicken swapped for napa cabbage and the soaked shiitake left over from making shiitake–kombu dashi, simmered in that same dashi. The cabbage stems go in first; the leaves only need a minute.

1 bowl~15 min (+ rice)
IngredientsMise en placePrep orderSteps

Ingredients

Main

  • Napa cabbage150 g (3–4 leaves); stems sliced 1 cm, leaves chopped, kept separate
  • Soaked shiitake3, sliced (from the dashi, or soak dried ones 30 min)
  • Eggs2
  • Cooked short-grain rice1 bowl (~200 g)

With rice already cooked and shiitake already soaked, it’s about 15 minutes.

Step by step

  1. 1Rice first

    ~45 min ahead

    For this step

    • Cooked short-grain rice1 bowl (~200 g)

    Have a bowl of hot rice ready before starting the pan — the egg won’t wait.

  2. 2Cut

    ~5 min

    For this step

    • Napa cabbage150 g (3–4 leaves); stems sliced 1 cm
    • Soaked shiitake3
    • Separate the napa stems from the leaves.
    • Slice the stems about 1 cm across and chop the leaves.
    • Squeeze the soaked shiitake, cut off the stems and slice the caps.

  3. 3Sauce and eggs

    ~2 min

    For this step

    • Eggs2
    • Mix the sauce:
      • shiitake–kombu dashi 80 ml
      • soy sauce 1 tbsp
      • mirin 1 tbsp
      • sake 1 tbsp
      • sugar 1 tsp
    • Beat the eggs loosely — a few strokes, streaks of white and yolk.

  4. 4Simmer stems and shiitake

    medium3–4 min
    • Sauce into the small pan with the napa stems and shiitake.
    • Simmer until the stems turn translucent.

  5. 5Leaves in, lid on

    medium1–2 min
    • Add the leaves, cover and cook until just wilted.
    • Napa gives off water — if the pan looks very wet, simmer uncovered for a minute to reduce before the egg.

  6. 6Pour ⅔ of the egg

    medium30–60 s
    • Drizzle about two-thirds of the egg in a circle over the vegetables.
    • Cover until the edges set but the top is still runny.

  7. 7Pour the rest, heat off

    heat off~45 s

    Pour the remaining egg, cook 10–15 seconds, turn off the heat and cover for 30 seconds — glossy and just set.

  8. 8Slide onto the rice

    For this step

    • Spring onion greensa few
    • Shichimi togarashioptional
    • Tilt the pan and slide everything, broth included, over the rice.
    • Spring onion greens and shichimi on top.

Vegetarian

With shiitake–kombu dashi it’s fully vegetarian (the eggs are the only animal product).

Amounts

Your notes didn’t include quantities — these mirror the oyakodon proportions, with 150 g napa standing in for the 150 g chicken.

No homemade dashi?

Water + ¼ tsp dashi powder, or the soaking water from the shiitake on its own.
