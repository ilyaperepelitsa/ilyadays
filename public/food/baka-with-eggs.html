Baka with eggs

Dawn’s breakfast stew (baka is beef in Tagalog). Brown the beef hard enough to leave a fond — the browned bits stuck to the pan — then let the tomatoes, onion and garlic lift it off. Freezer-friendly; best with jasmine or garlic rice.

~4 servings~3–3½ h, mostly hands-off
IngredientsMise en placePrep orderSteps

Ingredients

Stew

  • Stewing beef2 lb (~900 g), cubed
  • Roma tomatoes4, quartered
  • Yellow onion1 medium, diced
  • Garlic1 head, roughly diced
  • Fish sauce2–3 tsp, optional
  • Salt & pepperto taste
  • Boiled eggs4, peeled — see boiled eggs

Best served with

  • Jasmine rice or garlic ricefreshly cooked

  • Wooden spoon

Step by step

  1. 1Brown the beef

    medium-high~15 min

    For this step

    • Stewing beef2 lb (~900 g)
    • Salt & pepperto taste
    • Salt and pepper the beef.
    • Sear it over heat high enough that brown bits glue themselves to the bottom of the pot — that fond is where the flavour comes from.

  2. 2Deglaze with the vegetables

    medium~8 min
    • Lift the beef out and pour off part of the fat.
    • Tip into the hot pot to deglaze it:
      • roma tomatoes 4
      • yellow onion 1 medium
      • garlic 1 head
    • When the vegetables have given up their juices, return the beef and stir it through for about a minute.

  3. 3Stew low, covered

    low2–3 h
    • Lid on, heat low, for 2–3 hours.
    • Look in and stir every 15–30 minutes — more often if stews are new to you — making sure there’s still liquid and nothing is catching on the bottom.

  4. 4Reduce and season

    medium~15 min

    For this step

    • Fish sauce2–3 tsp
    • Take the lid off and let it cook down until the sauce is as thick as you like.
    • Stir in the fish sauce.

  5. 5Eggs in

    ~2 min

    For this step

    • Boiled eggs4, peeled — see boiled eggs

    Nestle in the boiled eggs and turn them until they’re coated.

  6. 6Serve or store

    For this step

    • Jasmine rice or garlic ricefreshly cooked
    • Serve hot over rice,
    • or cool it and keep it in the fridge or freezer.

Fond

  • The browned crust that sticks to the pot when meat sears hard.
  • Something wet — here the tomatoes — loosens it so it melts into the sauce.
