Chicken Pad Thai

A simplified home version: tangy tamarind, sweet-salty sauce, egg, crunchy peanuts and lime. No mirin, sake or sesame oil needed.

1 generous serving~20 min
IngredientsMise en placePrep orderSteps

Ingredients

Main

  • Dried flat rice noodles100 g
  • Chicken150 g, thinly sliced
  • Egg1
  • Garlic2 cloves, finely chopped
  • Spring onions2; whites sliced, greens cut into short lengths
  • Bean sprouts60–80 g
  • Neutral cooking oil1 tbsp (split in half)

Sauce — stir until the sugar dissolves

  • Tamarind purée1½ tbsp
  • Fish sauce1 tbsp
  • Brown or palm sugar1 tbsp
  • Warm water2 tbsp

To serve

  • Roasted peanuts15 g, roughly crushed
  • Lime½, cut into wedges
  • Chilli flakesoptional, to taste

Equipment

  • Large frying pan or wok
  • Clean plate

Step by step

  1. 1Noodles first

    per packet

    For this step

    • Dried flat rice noodles100 g
    • Follow the packet’s stir-fry preparation instructions, stopping while they still have a little firmness.
    • Drain well and use promptly; they’ll finish cooking in the sauce.
    • Avoid fully softening them beforehand or aggressively stirring them in the pan.

  2. 2Prep everything

    ~8 min

    For this step

    • Chicken150 g
    • Garlic2 cloves
    • Spring onions2; whites sliced
    • Roasted peanuts15 g
    • Lime½
    • Slice the chicken.
    • Chop the garlic; slice the spring onion whites and cut the greens into short lengths.
    • Crush the peanuts, cut the lime into wedges.
    • Stir the sauce until the sugar dissolves:
      • tamarind purée 1½ tbsp
      • fish sauce 1 tbsp
      • brown or palm sugar 1 tbsp
      • warm water 2 tbsp

  3. 3Chicken and aromatics

    medium-high~4 min
    • Heat half the oil over medium-high heat.
    • Fry the chicken until cooked through.
    • Add garlic and spring onion whites for the final 30 seconds, then transfer to a clean plate.

  4. 4Egg

    medium-high~1 min

    For this step

    • Egg1

    Add the remaining oil, crack in the egg and scramble until just set.

  5. 5Noodles and sauce

    medium-high2–3 min
    • Add the prepared noodles and sauce.
    • Gently fold over medium-high heat until the noodles are tender and most of the sauce is absorbed.
    • Add a little more water if necessary.

  6. 6Chicken, sprouts, greens

    medium-high~1 min

    For this step

    • Bean sprouts60–80 g

    Return the chicken, then fold in the bean sprouts and spring onion greens; cook until hot.

  7. 7Serve

    For this step

    • Chilli flakesoptional

    Serve with crushed peanuts, lime and optional chilli.

Buy tamarind purée/paste, not concentrate

  • Buy tamarind purée/paste rather than a highly concentrated extract: the strength varies, and strong concentrate cannot be substituted spoon for spoon.
  • The tamarind supplies the characteristic sourness.

No tamarind?

  • For a lime-based approximation, start with 1 tablespoon lime juice instead, then adjust after cooking.
  • It won’t taste identical, but lime is a workable fallback.
