Japanese cucumber pickle (sunomono)

Crisp and sweet-sour. Salting pulls the water out; rinsing takes the salt back off so the marinade does the seasoning. Ginger, garlic and a splash of rice vinegar go in first, then the vinegar–mirin–sugar marinade, with light soy and sesame at the very end. No rice vinegar for the marinade? Pick your vinegar below — the amounts update to match.

2 side servings~20 min + marinating

Step by step

  1. 1Slice thin

    ~3 min

    For this step

    • Cucumber250 g
    • Slice the cucumber about 2 mm thick (a mandoline makes this quick).
    • Peeling a few stripes off first looks nice but is optional.

  2. 2Salt and rest

    10 min

    For this step

    • Fine salt, for drawing out water½ tsp

    Toss with ½ tsp salt and leave 10 minutes to pull the water out.

  3. 3Rinse and squeeze

    ~2 min

    Rinse the salt off under cold water, then squeeze firmly by the handful — this is what keeps them crunchy and stops them watering down the marinade.

  4. 4Ginger, garlic, rice vinegar

    ~1 min

    For this step

    • Ginger, grated1 tsp
    • Garlic, grated1 small clove
    • Rice vinegar (first splash)1 tbsp

    Add the grated ginger and garlic and the 1 tbsp rice vinegar. Mix it up.

  5. 5The marinade

    ~2 min

    For this step

    • Rice vinegar2 tbsp (30 ml)
    • Water—
    • Stir until the sugar dissolves:
      • rice vinegar + water
      • mirin 1 tbsp
      • sugar 2 tsp
    • Pour it over the cucumbers and toss.
    • Optional: microwave the mirin and sugar together for 20–30 seconds first to dissolve the sugar and cook off the mirin’s alcohol; let it cool before adding.

  6. 6Rest, then soy and sesame

    10 min – 1 h

    For this step

    • Light soy sauce (usukuchi)½–1 tsp
    • Toasted sesame seeds1 tsp
    • Chill 10 minutes (crispest) up to 1 hour.
    • Add the light soy sauce and sesame at the end, just before serving.
    • Keeps 2–3 days in the fridge, softening as it sits.

Table vinegar 9%1 tbsp (15 ml)1 tbsp (15 ml)2½ tsp

White / distilled vinegar 5%1¾ tbsp (27 ml)½ tsp (3 ml)2½ tsp
Matched to rice vinegar’s ~4.5% acidity for the marinade. Non-rice vinegars are sharper and less rounded, hence the extra sugar — taste and adjust.

Reading Korean vinegar labels

  • 양조식초 (brewed vinegar, ~6–7%) is the one the 7% row means.
  • 2배식초 / 3배식초 are double / triple strength — use half / a third of the 7% amount and make up the rest with water.
  • 빙초산 is glacial acetic acid (~99%): never use it as-is.

Seasoned rice vinegar (sushi vinegar)?

  • It already contains sugar and salt.
  • Use 2 tbsp straight in the marinade, keep the mirin, and skip the sugar.

Light soy

  • Usukuchi (light) soy is saltier but paler than regular soy — it seasons without darkening the cucumber.
  • Regular soy works; use a little less.
