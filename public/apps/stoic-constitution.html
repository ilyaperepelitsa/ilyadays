The first five articles

All 418 passages of the Meditations were grouped by meaning into chapters and sections. Here the first five sections are each fused into an article. An article makes one statement, then looks at it from five fixed sides: why, in action, practice, when it breaks, and if I doubt it. On a wide screen the five sides stand next to one another as columns. The small numbers after words name the passages those words come from, and a double underline means more than one passage backs them. Hover for the exact quotes. Click or tap to read the passages next to what follows from each.

1 · From the whole book to topics

How the topics were found

Each passage was turned into a vector of numbers that stands for its meaning. Passages close to one another were merged step by step into a tree. Cutting the tree high gives chapters; cutting each chapter again gives sections. The names are the editor’s, written after reading.
